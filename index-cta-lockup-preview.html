The Haas Relationship

What being the authorized outlet means.

Haas Automation runs an exclusive outlet model: one authorized partner per territory. In Michigan, that partner is Gerotech, and every part of the Haas ownership experience runs through one team.

01 · Sales

The full Haas line, one source.

Mills, lathes, 5-axis machines, automation, and tooling — sold, demoed, and quoted directly through the factory outlet.

02 · Application Support

Factory-backed engineering.

On-staff engineers match the right Haas machine to the job and prove out the process before it hits your floor.

03 · Warranty

Coverage owned locally.

Warranty work is handled by the same outlet that sold the machine — no routing customers back to the factory.

04 · Service & Parts

Factory-trained technicians.

Certified Haas service, genuine parts, and training keep machines running across Flat Rock and Grand Rapids territories.
